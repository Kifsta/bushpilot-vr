BUSH PILOT VR - POSE LAB

In the headset: press ENTER VR (bottom of the page). Your hand holds the item. Squeeze the GRIP on your OTHER hand and move it: the item moves and turns with it. Sticks nudge it. Point and pull the trigger on the big panel buttons. SAVE, then COPY CODE.

On a PC: drag the arrows / rings on the item (T = move, R = rotate, Ctrl+Z = undo, drag empty space to look around). Click the panels below just like in VR.

Load a pose: paste a code (from VR Dev or from your saved list) and press LOAD.
