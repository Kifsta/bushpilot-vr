# Bush Pilot VR sound credits (sfx/)

Every sound here is **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)** or was made for the game. No attribution is required; the authors are credited anyway.
Downloaded only over HTTPS from kenney.nl (its own signed download links) and opengameart.org (file links on each CC0 page). Every file was checked as
audio (magic bytes + ffprobe: exactly one audio stream) and never executed; zips were listed first and only audio extracted. Processed with ffmpeg / numpy:
trimmed, mono, loudness-normalised, loops crossfaded into themselves, Ogg Opus 28-48 kb/s. Full CC0 legal text: LICENSES.
