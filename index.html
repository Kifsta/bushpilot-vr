Reload: grip the ammo pouch on your right hip = one cartridge, touch its tip to the loading gate (right side of the receiver). Belt: knife sheath left hip, 2 pouches front, rifle sling over the right shoulder - let go of a thing near its slot (green box) to holster it. Pack: grip over either shoulder, pull it round to the front; let go = it hovers open at your chest. Drop items in, grip minis to take out. Push it back over your shoulder (or A/X on the free hand) to wear it. Let go of items anywhere else = they drop on the ground. Knife: stab zombies (head = kill), slice across a kill 3x (or hold grip on it with the knife in the other hand) = meat.
