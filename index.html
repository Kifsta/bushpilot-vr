BUSH PILOT VR v0.72 - PA-18 Super Cub bush delivery: take jobs off the strip boards, load and strap the cargo, fly it out. Fragile, perishable and live cargo, bush sites, deer hunting jobs, airdrops, running costs (fuel, oil, wear, repairs), reputation with every customer (better pay, requests, the Halvorsen board), plane upgrades from the hangar parts board (long-range tank, tundra tyres, cargo pod, LED light, COM radio), townsfolk with their own stories, medevac and search-and-rescue call-outs. No wind, no weather. You start on foot by the Cub at the cabin strip. Walk up to her and climb in (F / Y), then start her up off the checklist card (J). Desktop - flying: W/S or arrows pitch, A/D or arrows roll, Q/E rudder, Shift / Ctrl (or + / -) throttle, B brakes, [ ] flaps, PgUp / PgDn trim, R reset to the strip (cold and dark), F climb out, M map, L landing light, Home recentre, mouse looks (click to capture). Desktop - engine: U fuel valve, O master, Y mixture, H carb heat, P primer, I mags (OFF > R > L > BOTH), hold Space = starter, K panel lights, 1-4 windows / door, J checklist card, N clipboard (, and . turn its pages: JOBS, W&B, CUB, PERF). Desktop - on foot: WASD walk, Shift run, C crouch, Space hop, F climb in, G refuel at the strip pump (near a wing filler cap), M map. Jobs: 1-3 at a job board accept / cancel, E pick up / set down cargo (E by the Cub loads or unloads it), / seat belt + cargo net. Hunting: T take / sling / rack the rifle, LMB fire, RMB aim, X lever, Z load a round (by the ammo box: buy 20 for $10; at the Dry Fork hangar's HARDWARE counter: $35 a box, Z again takes it), V binoculars, hold E by a downed deer to field dress it. Airdrops: in flight open the right door (1 upper window, 2 lower door), G pushes the next bundle out - drop it on the smoke / X. Running costs: fuel is paid at the red drums (4 strips, 4 prices); oil, tyres + brakes, repairs and the 100-hour service at the home HANGAR board (1-4 there). VR - in the cockpit: grip the stick (right) and throttle (left), left stick = rudder, left trigger = brakes, reach up and pull the flap handle down a notch at a time, wind the trim crank, turn knobs / the mag key / the primer / the fuel valve with your hand, poke the rocker and toggles. Grip the clipboard on your thigh to lift it, its hand's trigger turns the page. A / X recentre, B reset, Y climb out. Turn your left wrist to your face and poke its MENU button with your right finger for SETTINGS. VR - on foot: left stick walks (click = run), right stick turns (snap / smooth in Settings), right stick down = crouch, up = hop, Y climb in, grab a wing filler cap at the home strip to refuel from the pump, grab the map off its cockpit holder. Jobs: poke ACCEPT on a strip's job board, grip cargo to carry it, let go over the rear seat / baggage bay, grab the belt buckle / net to strap. Hunting: grip the rifle's wrist (cabin / Cub rack), other hand on the forend, trigger fires, pinch the lever loop and swing it (or flick), rounds from the belt pouch into the gate; binoculars on your chest (one hand or both, a hand on each barrel), raise them to your eyes; grip and hold a downed deer to dress it. Airdrops: unlatch + open both halves of the right door, reach back and grip a bundle, put your hand out through the door and let go. Running costs: grab a wing cap by any strip's red drum to buy fuel; poke a price on the hangar / fuel boards for oil, tyres, repairs, the 100-hour.
