BUSH PILOT VR v0.51 - PA-18 Super Cub bush delivery: take jobs off the strip boards, load and strap the cargo, fly it out. Fragile, perishable and live cargo. No wind, no weather. You start on foot by the Cub at the cabin strip. Walk up to her and climb in (F / Y), then start her up off the checklist card (J). Desktop - flying: W/S or arrows pitch, A/D or arrows roll, Q/E rudder, Shift / Ctrl (or + / -) throttle, B brakes, [ ] flaps, PgUp / PgDn trim, R reset to the strip (cold and dark), F climb out, M map, L landing light, Home recentre, mouse looks (click to capture). Desktop - engine: U fuel valve, O master, Y mixture, H carb heat, P primer, I mags (OFF > R > L > BOTH), hold Space = starter, K panel lights, 1-4 windows / door, J checklist card. Desktop - on foot: WASD walk, Shift run, C crouch, Space hop, F climb in, G refuel at the strip pump (near a wing filler cap), M map. Jobs: 1-3 at a job board accept / cancel, E pick up / set down cargo (E by the Cub loads or unloads it), / seat belt + cargo net. VR - in the cockpit: grip the stick (right) and throttle (left), left stick = rudder, left trigger = brakes, reach up and pull the flap handle down a notch at a time, wind the trim crank, turn knobs / the mag key / the primer / the fuel valve with your hand, poke the rocker and toggles. A / X recentre, B reset, Y climb out. Poke the screen on your left wrist with your right finger for SETTINGS. VR - on foot: left stick walks (click = run), right stick turns (snap / smooth in Settings), right stick down = crouch, up = hop, Y climb in, grab a wing filler cap at the home strip to refuel from the pump, grab the map off its cockpit holder. Jobs: poke ACCEPT on a strip's job board, grip cargo to carry it, let go over the rear seat / baggage bay, grab the belt buckle / net to strap. Settings (` or F2 on desktop, the wrist board in VR): graphics, comfort, FPS counter, stick on release, control labels, haptics, time of day.
